# Humanitarian Data Analysis Report: Rice Prices in Bihar

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes rice price data collected across Bihar state during July 15 - August 14, 2023. The analysis reveals significant geographic price disparities across 10 monitored markets, with prices ranging from 32.0 to 42.08 INR/KG. The average price across monitored markets is **37.17 INR/KG**, with a price volatility of 31.5% indicating potential accessibility and affordability challenges in certain regions.

---

## Key Findings

### Price Distribution
- **Average rice price (monitored markets):** 37.17 INR/KG
- **Price range:** 32.0 - 42.08 INR/KG
- **Price differential:** 10.08 INR/KG (31.5% volatility)
- **Data period:** July 15 - August 14, 2023
- **Markets monitored:** 10 locations across Bihar

### Geographic Price Clustering

**Higher-priced markets:**
- Khagaria (42.08 INR/KG) — highest
- Muzaffarpur (39.81 INR/KG)
- Bhagalpur (38.77 INR/KG)
- Madhubani (38.17 INR/KG)
- Patna (38.0 INR/KG)

**Lower-priced markets:**
- Gaya (32.0 INR/KG) — lowest
- Munger (32.19 INR/KG)
- Katihar (33.14 INR/KG)
- Purnia (36.52 INR/KG)
- Darbhanga (37.07 INR/KG)

---

## Risk Assessment

### Price Volatility Risk
The 31.5% variance between highest and lowest prices indicates:
- **Accessibility disparities:** Consumers in Khagaria face 31.3% higher costs compared to Gaya
- **Potential supply constraints:** Northern districts (Khagaria, Madhubani, Muzaffarpur) show elevated prices, suggesting possible supply chain inefficiencies
- **Affordability concerns:** The price spread may disproportionately impact vulnerable populations in higher-priced markets

### Geographic Risk Zones
- **High-risk (affordability):** Khagaria district shows the highest prices, potentially limiting household food security
- **Lower-risk (price):** Gaya and Munger offer more affordable options but may face supply availability or quality concerns

### Data Limitations
- **Temporal scope:** Analysis limited to one-month period; seasonal trends not captured
- **Market coverage:** Only 10 markets monitored; analysis may not represent all 38 districts in Bihar
- **No retail variation data:** Analysis does not differentiate between wholesale, retail, or fair-price shop rates
- **No quality data:** Prices not stratified by rice variety, grade, or quality metrics

---

## Recommendations

### 1. **Market Stabilization Measures**
- Investigate supply chain barriers in high-price districts (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur)
- Strengthen fair-price shop (PDS) reach in above-average price markets to provide subsidized alternatives

### 2. **Expanded Monitoring**
- Extend data collection beyond 10 markets to cover all major trading centers
- Implement weekly price tracking to detect volatility trends and seasonal fluctuations
- Disaggregate data by rice type (common, basmati, fine varieties) and retail channel

### 3. **Targeted Interventions**
- Direct food assistance or price subsidies in high-volatility zones to protect vulnerable populations
- Strengthen agricultural supply chains in northern districts experiencing price premiums
- Coordinate with public distribution system to optimize coverage in Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, and Bhagalpur

### 4. **Data Enhancement**
- Include quality and variety metrics in future price tracking
- Collect data on household purchasing power relative to local prices
- Monitor correlation between market prices and food security indicators
