# Humanitarian Data Analysis Report: Rice Price Situation in Bihar

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes rice price data from 10 districts across Bihar state. The analysis reveals significant geographic price disparities, with prices ranging from 32.0 to 42.08 INR/kg—a variation of 31.5% across districts. Historical data indicates prices have doubled over the tracked period. While the data provides insights into regional price patterns, **critical limitations exist regarding data currency and completeness** that constrain actionable humanitarian assessment.

---

## Key Findings

### Price Distribution and Volatility
- **Current price range:** 32.0 INR/kg (Gaya) to 42.08 INR/kg (Khagaria)
- **Geographic spread:** 10.08 INR/kg difference across monitored districts
- **Historical increase:** 100% price escalation from 22.0 to 44.0 INR/kg over unspecified tracking period
- **Coefficient of variation:** 31.5% indicates substantial inter-district volatility

### Regional Disparities
**High-price zones (Northeast):**
- Khagaria: 42.08 INR/kg
- Muzaffarpur: 39.81 INR/kg

**Low-price zones (South):**
- Gaya: 32.0 INR/kg
- Munger: 32.19 INR/kg

**Mid-range zones (Central):**
- Patna, Darbhanga, Purnia, Katihar, Bhagalpur, Madhubani: 33.14–38.77 INR/kg

### Data Limitations and Gaps

**Critical limitation identified:** All price data originates from **July-August 2023**. This represents a significant gap:
- Current market conditions are unknown
- Seasonal price fluctuations are not captured
- Recent supply chain disruptions or policy changes are not reflected
- Temporal relevance for humanitarian decision-making is compromised

---

## Risk Assessment

| Risk Factor | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Geographic price inequality** | High | 31.5% variation suggests unequal market access and affordability across districts |
| **Supply chain inefficiency** | Medium-High | Northern districts show 30% higher prices than southern districts; suggests distribution or logistics constraints |
| **Food security affordability** | Medium | Doubling of historical prices indicates potential purchasing power erosion for vulnerable populations |
| **Data currency** | Critical | July-August 2023 baseline; no current price trajectories available |
| **Incomplete temporal analysis** | High | Unable to assess seasonal trends, volatility patterns, or current stability |

**Vulnerable populations at risk:** Low-income households in high-price districts (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur) face disproportionate food security challenges.

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions
1. **Update data collection:** Acquire current rice price data from all 10 districts to establish present-day baseline and identify price trend direction
2. **Temporal granularity:** Obtain weekly or bi-weekly price monitoring data to capture seasonal fluctuations and volatility patterns
3. **Supply chain investigation:** Conduct rapid assessment of logistics, storage, and distribution constraints in high-price zones (particularly Khagaria and Muzaffarpur)

### Analytical Priorities
4. **Purchasing power parity analysis:** Cross-reference rice prices with local wage data and household income in affected districts to quantify food security risk
5. **Price volatility modeling:** Establish trend lines using monthly data to predict affordability crises
6. **Market access mapping:** Identify barriers (transportation, market concentration, hoarding) causing 31.5% inter-district disparity

### Policy-Level Actions
7. **Targeted price stabilization:** Implement district-specific interventions in Khagaria and Muzaffarpur where prices exceed 39 INR/kg
8. **Social protection calibration:** Design food security programs (PDS enhancement, cash transfers) scaled to regional price differentials
9. **Supply chain optimization:** Prioritize infrastructure improvements in high-disparity corridors

### Monitoring Framework
10. **Establish real-time tracking:** Implement monthly rice price monitoring across all 10 districts with data lag not exceeding 4 weeks
11. **Trigger-based alerts:** Set price threshold alerts (e.g., >45 INR/kg) to activate humanitarian response protocols

---

## Conclusion

While the analysis confirms significant geographic rice price disparities in Bihar, **the July-August 2023 data source severely limits current situational awareness and humanitarian planning relevance**. Updated, granular price data is essential before operational recommendations can be reliably implemented. Immediate data refresh is prerequisite to any evidence-based response.
