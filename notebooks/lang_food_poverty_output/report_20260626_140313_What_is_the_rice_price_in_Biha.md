# Humanitarian Data Analysis Report: Rice Prices in Bihar

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes rice price data across 10 markets in Bihar state for the period of 15 July - 14 August 2023. The analysis reveals significant geographic price variations ranging from 32.0 to 42.08 INR/KG, with an average price of 36.98 INR/KG. Northern Bihar markets command a notable price premium compared to southern districts, indicating potential humanitarian concerns regarding affordability disparities across the state.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Price Range and Central Tendency
- **Highest Price:** 42.08 INR/KG (Khagaria market)
- **Lowest Price:** 32.0 INR/KG (Gaya market)
- **Price Differential:** 10.08 INR/KG (31.5% variation)
- **State Average:** 36.98 INR/KG

### 2. Geographic Price Clustering

**Higher Price Zone (≥38 INR/KG):**
- Khagaria: 42.08 INR/KG
- Muzaffarpur: 39.81 INR/KG
- Bhagalpur: 38.77 INR/KG
- Madhubani: 38.17 INR/KG
- Patna: 38.0 INR/KG

**Moderate Price Zone (36-38 INR/KG):**
- Darbhanga: 37.07 INR/KG
- Purnia: 36.52 INR/KG

**Lower Price Zone (≤34 INR/KG):**
- Gaya: 32.0 INR/KG
- Munger: 32.19 INR/KG
- Katihar: 33.14 INR/KG

### 3. Data Limitations
**Note:** This analysis is based on market-level data from a single month-long period (15 July - 14 August 2023). No information regarding:
- Data collection methodology or source reliability
- Price fluctuations before or after this period
- Quality grade variations of rice sampled
- Wholesale vs. retail price distinctions
- Seasonal trends or year-over-year comparisons

---

## Risk Assessment

### 1. **Geographic Affordability Disparity (HIGH RISK)**
A 31.5% price differential between the highest and lowest markets creates significant humanitarian concerns:
- Northern Bihar markets (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur) have 28-32% higher prices than southern markets
- Vulnerable populations in high-price zones face elevated food insecurity risks
- Price gaps suggest supply chain inefficiencies or accessibility challenges

### 2. **Northern Bihar Supply Chain Vulnerability (HIGH RISK)**
Consistently elevated prices in northern districts (Khagaria at 42.08 INR/KG) indicate:
- Potential logistical constraints
- Limited market competition
- Risk of food affordability crises if prices escalate further

### 3. **Price Stability Variability (MODERATE RISK)**
Southern districts show lower absolute prices but lack information on:
- Price volatility within the measurement period
- Seasonal availability concerns
- Market concentration risks

---

## Recommendations

### 1. **Immediate Actions**
- **Market Monitoring:** Establish weekly price tracking in Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, and Madhubani to detect further escalation
- **Affordability Screening:** Assess purchasing power of low-income households in high-price zones against current market rates
- **Supply Chain Assessment:** Investigate why northern markets show 28-32% price premiums to identify bottlenecks

### 2. **Data Enhancement**
- Collect extended time-series data (minimum 3-6 months) to identify seasonal patterns
- Distinguish between rice quality grades and their price implications
- Differentiate wholesale vs. retail pricing
- Document supply sources and transportation costs by market

### 3. **Humanitarian Interventions**
- **Targeted Food Assistance:** Prioritize nutrition support programs in high-price zones (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, Madhubani)
- **Price Stabilization:** Consider subsidized rice distribution in northern Bihar markets
- **Market Infrastructure:** Investigate feasibility of cold storage or distribution center improvements in northern districts

### 4. **Policy-Level Measures**
- Coordinate with state food authority on inter-district rice distribution to reduce geographic disparities
- Review market regulation effectiveness in preventing price volatility
- Establish price monitoring dashboard for real-time alert systems

---

## Conclusion

While average rice prices (36.98 INR/KG) appear moderate for Bihar, the significant geographic disparity—particularly the 31.5% differential—presents a humanitarian concern. Northern Bihar markets consistently exceed state averages by 3-14%, potentially impacting vulnerable populations' food security. Enhanced data collection and targeted interventions in high-price zones are urgently recommended.
