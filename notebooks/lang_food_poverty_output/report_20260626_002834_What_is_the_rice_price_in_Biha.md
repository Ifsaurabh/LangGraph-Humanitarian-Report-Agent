# Humanitarian Data Analysis Report: Rice Prices in Bihar

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes rice pricing data across Bihar state during the reference period of July 15 - August 14, 2023. The analysis reveals significant geographic price variation across 10 districts, with prices ranging from 32.0 INR/kg to 42.08 INR/kg. The 31.5% price differential across districts indicates potential market inefficiencies and supply chain disparities that warrant attention for food security and humanitarian considerations.

---

## Key Findings

### Price Range and Distribution
- **Highest Price:** Khagaria district at 42.08 INR/kg
- **Lowest Price:** Gaya district at 32.0 INR/kg
- **Price Spread:** 10.08 INR/kg difference
- **Mean District Price:** 36.76 INR/kg
- **Data Coverage:** 10 districts analyzed

### Geographic Clustering Patterns

**Higher Price Zones (≥38 INR/kg):**
- Khagaria: 42.08 INR/kg
- Muzaffarpur: 39.81 INR/kg
- Bhagalpur: 38.77 INR/kg
- Madhubani: 38.17 INR/kg
- Patna: 38.0 INR/kg

**Lower Price Zones (≤33.14 INR/kg):**
- Gaya: 32.0 INR/kg
- Munger: 32.19 INR/kg
- Katihar: 33.14 INR/kg

**Mid-Range Districts (36-37 INR/kg):**
- Darbhanga: 37.07 INR/kg
- Purnia: 36.52 INR/kg

---

## Risk Assessment

### Food Security Implications
1. **Affordability Disparity:** The 31.5% price variation creates unequal access to staple food across districts. Communities in Khagaria face significantly higher food costs than those in Gaya.

2. **Geographic Vulnerability:** Northern and eastern districts (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur) demonstrate systematically higher prices, potentially indicating:
   - Supply chain inefficiencies
   - Limited market accessibility
   - Local demand pressures
   - Higher transportation costs

3. **Vulnerable Population Impact:** Lower-income households in high-price zones face disproportionate food security risks.

### Data Limitations
- **Temporal Scope:** Data limited to single month (July 15 - August 14, 2023); does not reflect seasonal variations or price trends
- **Geographic Coverage:** Only 10 districts analyzed; incomplete representation of all Bihar districts
- **Price Type:** Retail price data only; wholesale and institutional prices not analyzed
- **Market Data:** No information on supply volumes, demand patterns, or stock availability
- **Currency Stability:** Analysis does not account for inflation or currency fluctuations beyond the study period

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Market Stabilization Measures
- Investigate supply chain bottlenecks in high-price zones (Khagaria, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur)
- Establish direct procurement channels to reduce intermediary markups in northern districts
- Improve transportation infrastructure to northern/eastern districts

### 2. Food Security Interventions
- Prioritize subsidized rice distribution programs in districts with prices ≥39 INR/kg
- Implement price monitoring systems across all districts for early warning
- Establish strategic grain reserves in high-price districts

### 3. Data Enhancement
- Expand geographic coverage to include all Bihar districts
- Establish continuous price monitoring (weekly or bi-weekly) to track trends
- Collect qualitative data on factors driving price variations
- Include wholesale prices and availability data
- Monitor seasonal price fluctuations over 12-month periods

### 4. Further Analysis Required
- Correlation analysis between district prices and local agricultural production
- Supply chain mapping for high-price zones
- Income and affordability analysis relative to local wage rates
- Comparative analysis with national and regional benchmarks
